Part A · required

Confidentiality agreement

Between NeuroResilience Consulting Inc. (“we”) and you, the founding member named at the bottom.

A1. What is confidentialRead ✓

While you are in the founding round, please keep these private:

  • Unreleased screens, features and designs, including screenshots and recordings.
  • The founding round price and terms.
  • The study materials: the psychological safety learning session and the recall questions.
  • Anything we mark as confidential when we send it to you.
  • Other participants’ names, if you learn them.

The study materials matter most. If future participants see the learning session or the questions in advance, their results are worthless and the whole study is compromised. Please do not share them with anyone, including people you know who might take part later.

A2. What is not confidentialRead ✓

You are free to say whatever you think of Axis. This agreement covers unreleased material, not your opinion.

  • Your own honest views, good or bad, including in the feedback survey.
  • The fact that you are taking part.
  • Anything already public, or that becomes public without you breaking this agreement.
  • Anything you already knew, or work out independently.
  • Anything you are required to disclose by law or by a court.

Nothing here stops you from speaking to your doctor, your lawyer, a regulator, a privacy commissioner or a research ethics body, or from reporting a concern about safety or about how you were treated. You do not need our permission, and you do not need to tell us.

A3. How long it lastsRead ✓
  • Unreleased features and prices: until they are released publicly, or [two] years from signing, whichever comes first.
  • Study materials: until the study closes and the findings are published, or [two] years, whichever comes first.

If you leave the founding round or ask for a refund, this section still applies for the period above, because the reason for it does not change.

A4. Who owns whatRead ✓

We own Axis, including the app, the designs and the study materials. Being a founding member does not give you any ownership of them, and your licence to use Axis is personal to you.

You own your own data: what you enter, your logs and your answers. You can ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it.

If you send us an idea or a suggestion, we may use it to improve Axis without owing you anything, and without naming you. If you would rather not have an idea used that way, do not send it.

A5. If something goes wrongRead ✓

If confidential material gets out by accident, tell us as soon as you can and help us limit it. We would rather hear about it early than find out later.

This agreement is governed by the laws of Nova Scotia and the laws of Canada that apply there.

Part B · optional

The retention study

This part is yours to decline. Your purchase, your access and your refund rights are the same either way, and we will not ask why.

B1. What the study asks, and what you would doRead ✓

Most workplace training is forgotten within days. We want to know whether rest, nutrition and movement change how much a person remembers from a session on psychological safety the next morning.

  • One evening: a short learning session, about 10 minutes, then a brief recall check.
  • Overnight: follow the pillars you are assigned. Groups are assigned at random, and some people are asked to change nothing.
  • The next morning: a second recall check, about 10 minutes.

About 20 to 25 minutes in total.

B2. What we collect, and how it is protectedRead ✓
  • Your answers on the two recall checks, which pillars you were assigned, and whether you followed them.
  • Your name and email, to send the second session and your results.
  • [Anything else, listed exactly.]

We do not collect medical records or diagnoses.

  • Answers are stored under a code, separately from your name.
  • Results are reported as group averages only, never as individual results.
  • Nothing is published for any group with fewer than five people.
  • Only [names or roles] can see data that identifies you. Data is kept until [date], then deleted.
  • Your employer is never told whether you took part, or how you scored.

We may publish anonymised, aggregated findings, including to organisations considering the program. Nothing identifying you is shared or sold.

B3. Stopping, risks and resultsRead ✓

You can withdraw at any point, without a reason, with no effect on anything else. If you ask, we delete your data, up to the point where results have already been combined and published.

The session covers how people are treated at work, which some people find uncomfortable. You can stop at any point.

There is no guaranteed benefit to you. Axis and this study are educational, and nothing here diagnoses or treats any condition.

We send the findings to every participant by [date], whatever they show, including a finding of no difference.
